# Mnemonic Rules for LangoSoft

Derived from iterative feedback during the LangoSoft session. Follow every rule before writing or approving a mnemonic.

---

## The Pattern

Every mnemonic has exactly **two CAPS words** in a short scene:

> **[CAPS phonetic anchor] + [CAPS translation] + minimal connecting scene**

- **CAPS phonetic anchor** — a common English word that *sounds like* the Italian word
- **CAPS translation** — the English meaning of the Italian word
- The scene is just background: a situation where the anchor produces or relates to the meaning

The two CAPS words are the mnemonic. The sentence is just glue.

**Good examples:**
> `acqua` → "A duck goes QUACK in WATER." — QUACK ≈ acqua, WATER = meaning
> `dolore` → "A DOLLAR stolen — deep GRIEF / PAIN." — DOLLAR ≈ dolore, GRIEF/PAIN = meaning

---

## Rule 1: Anchor word must be a real, common English word

Target vocabulary: **B1–B2 level**. The user is not a native English speaker.

**Forbidden anchor words (examples of what was rejected):**
- Too obscure: AERIE, MOGUL, FILIAL, VEDIC, LACHRIMAL, PELAGIC, INTROIT, COMPUNCTION, BEATITUDE, ATTRITION, IMPIOUS, FIE, GENTRY, PERDITION, PLANGENT, SEMPITERNAL, PALLBEARERS, FURORE, SAPIENT

**Test:** Would a B1 English learner know this word immediately? If not, replace it.

---

## Rule 2: No fake phonetic spellings

The anchor must be a real word, never a transcription of sounds.

| ❌ Fake | ✓ Real |
|--------|--------|
| PAH-CHAY | PATCHES |
| TAH-CHAY | ATTACHED |
| AEH-reh | AIR |
| OO-SHEE-TOE | SHOO |
| VOL-ya | VOLCANO |
| DEE-CHESTY | CHEST |

Hyphenated pairs are only acceptable if **both sides are real English words** (e.g., ON-DAY, SIR-AH, PRE-ARRANGED).

---

## Rule 3: No foreign/Latin/French/Spanish anchor words

Even if the word exists in English dictionaries, avoid it if its origin is non-English and it is not commonly known.

| ❌ Forbidden | Why |
|-------------|-----|
| AQUA | Latin, not basic English |
| AMOR | Latin/Italian |
| ANIMA | Latin |
| DOLOR | Latin/Spanish |
| VOILÀ | French |
| ESPERANZA | Spanish |
| TRATTORIA | Italian |
| ALIUS | Latin |

**Exception:** Words genuinely absorbed into everyday English (INFERNO, PIANO, VOLCANO) are fine.

**Famous brand names and proper nouns are also allowed** as long as they are not circular (i.e., not the Italian word itself):

| ✓ Allowed | Why |
|-----------|-----|
| GUCCI | world-famous brand, instantly visual |
| FERRARI | same |
| MOSES | biblical figure everyone knows |
| FIAT | absorbed into English |

The banned DOLCE (for `dolce`) and AMORE (for `amore`) were rejected because they ARE the Italian word — not because they are brands or proper nouns.

---

## Rule 4: No circular mnemonics

Do not use the Italian word itself (or a near-identical form) as the English anchor.

| ❌ Circular | Problem |
|------------|---------|
| TUTTI-FRUITY for `tutti` | tutti = the Italian word |
| AMORE for `amore` | same word |
| DOLCE (D&G brand) for `dolce` | same word |
| AQUA for `acqua` | same word, Latin |

---

## Rule 5: Anchor word must actually sound like the Italian

Check Italian pronunciation rules before choosing an anchor:

| Italian spelling | Sound | ✓ Anchor examples | ❌ Wrong anchors |
|-----------------|-------|------------------|-----------------|
| c + e/i | "ch" (church) | CHERRY, CHEST, CHEETAH | CERTIFY (s-sound) |
| c + a/o/u | "k" | KEY, CAKE, KING | — |
| ch | "k" | KEY, PARK | CHEF (sh-sound) |
| g + e/i | "j" | JAY, JUDGE, LEDGER | GEAR (hard g) |
| gn | "ny" | CANYON, ONION | SIGN (silent g) |
| gli | "ly" | MILLION, LILY | — |
| sc + e/i | "sh" | SHELL, DISH | SCIENCE (s-sound) |
| h | silent | AH, OH | HA, HO (audible h) |

---

## Rule 6: No "X means Y" redundancy

Never explicitly state the connection. The scene should imply it.

| ❌ Redundant | ✓ Better |
|-------------|---------|
| "acqua is WATER" | "A duck goes QUACK in WATER." |
| "aere is AIR" | "Fresh AIR in the open sky — ATMOSPHERE." |
| "JUSTICE — almost the same word: JUSTICE served" | "JUDGE delivers JUSTICE — fair and firm." |
| "AQUA is the same word: WATER" | (deleted — too obvious) |

---

## Rule 7: If a word is an obvious cognate, skip or keep minimal

If the Italian word already sounds exactly like its English meaning, no mnemonic is needed or a one-line note suffices. Do not write a story explaining what is self-evident.

---

## Rule 8: Prefer concrete, visual nouns as anchors

Abstract words make poor anchors because they produce no mental image.

| ❌ Abstract anchor | ✓ Concrete anchor |
|-------------------|------------------|
| MORE (for amor) | LOVE |
| FULL | BUCKET, CUP |
| GREAT | GIANT, MOUNTAIN |

---

## Rule 9: Keep sentences short and simple

- Max 1–2 short sentences
- One clear scene
- No academic language, no multi-clause explanations
- Inflected base form in parentheses at start: `(cammino) A CAMEL...`

## Rule 11: The scene must be a real scenario — not just a label

"[ANCHOR]: [TRANSLATION]" is not a mnemonic. The scene must show a situation where the anchor word produces, causes, or relates to the meaning.

| ❌ Label only | ✓ Real scene |
|--------------|-------------|
| `DOLLY: SAD / SORROWFUL` | `DO LENT: SAD / SORROWFUL — the fast leaves the heart heavy.` |
| `FACTORY: MAKER` | `FACTORY owner: the MAKER / CREATOR of all.` |
| `DOLLAR: PAIN` | `A DOLLAR stolen — deep GRIEF / PAIN fills the heart.` |

Gold standard: `città` → "A CHEETAH dashing through the CITY." — vivid action, both CAPS words visible, no redundancy.

## Rule 13: No em-dashes (—), semicolons, or label colons. Every mnemonic must be a sentence with a subject and a verb.

Em-dashes, semicolons, and `CAPS: meaning` colons all create lazy label format. None of them are stories. All three are banned.

| ❌ Half-baked | ✓ Story |
|--------------|--------|
| `PUNCTUAL arrival at the exact POINT / MOMENT.` | `A PUNCTUAL train hits the exact POINT and the crowd marks THAT MOMENT.` |
| `POET — verse-maker, a speaker of truth.` | `A POET climbs the cliff and shouts TRUTH into the wind below.` |
| `SAVAGE: WILD / SAVAGE and untamed.` | `A SAVAGE beast runs WILD and SAVAGE, completely untamed and free.` |
| `AUTHOR: the CREATOR of the story.` | `The AUTHOR writes alone at night and becomes the CREATOR of every story.` |

Every mnemonic must answer: **who does what?** If you can't name an actor performing an action, rewrite it.

## Rule 12: Two phonetic anchors are better than one when they cover the full word

When two common English words together cover all the syllables of the Italian word, use both as phonetic anchors — the scene connects them to the meaning.

| Italian | Syllables | Anchors | Scene |
|---------|-----------|---------|-------|
| dolente (doh-LEN-teh) | doh + lent | DO + LENT | `DO LENT: SAD / SORROWFUL` |
| perduta (per-DOO-tah) | per + doo-tah | PEAR + DUTY | `A PEAR on DUTY — LOST` |
| dolore (doh-LOR-eh) | dol + or | DOLLAR | single word covers both |

---

## Rule 10: Maximize syllable coverage

The anchor must match **as many syllables of the Italian word as possible**, not just the first one.

| Italian | ❌ Weak (1 syllable) | ✓ Better (2+ syllables) |
|---------|---------------------|------------------------|
| dolore (doh-LOR-eh) | DULL | DOLLAR |
| perduta (per-DOO-tah) | PURR | PRODUCE, PURDUE |
| lasciate (las-CHA-teh) | LASS | LATCH |

If you can only match one syllable, it's acceptable — but always search for a word that covers more first.

---

## Checklist before writing

- [ ] Anchor word is B1–B2 common English
- [ ] Anchor word is a real word (not a phonetic transcription)
- [ ] Anchor word is not Latin/French/Spanish/Italian
- [ ] Anchor word is not the Italian word itself
- [ ] Anchor word sounds like the Italian (check pronunciation rules above)
- [ ] Anchor word covers as many syllables as possible (not just the first)
- [ ] No explicit "X means Y" statement
- [ ] Scene is concrete and visual
- [ ] Sentence is short
- [ ] Exactly two CAPS words minimum: one = sound, one = meaning (two phonetic anchors OK when they cover the full word)
- [ ] Scene is a real scenario, not just "[ANCHOR]: [TRANSLATION]"
- [ ] Famous brands and proper nouns (GUCCI, MOSES) are OK if not circular
